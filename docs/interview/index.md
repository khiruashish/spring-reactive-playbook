## How to use these

- **Candidates:** answer aloud first, then expand the answer. If you can't explain a *why*, revisit
  the linked playbook page.
- **Interviewers:** the "Follow-ups" lines are where good answers separate from memorised ones. Ask for
  trade-offs, failure modes and production stories.

!!! tip "What strong answers have in common"
    They mention **subscription and demand**, name the **thread** something runs on, think about
    **failure and cancellation**, and know **when not** to use reactive.
